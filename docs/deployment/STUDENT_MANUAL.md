# Student User Manual — v1.0

1. Sign in with your student username and password.
2. Open **My courses** to see courses in which you are enrolled and their assignments; choose an assignment to inspect its details and your own deadline.
3. Click **Submit or resubmit a file...** to select your work. Supported file extensions are PDF, DOC, DOCX, TXT and ZIP. Confirm that the status/confirmation appears.
4. Before your deadline and before marking, you may resubmit; the latest version counts and previous versions remain recorded. Work submitted after your own deadline is accepted as **Late** but cannot be resubmitted.
5. Late penalties: one started late day costs 10% of the assignment maximum, two days 20%, three days 30%; submissions later than 72 hours receive a final mark of zero.
6. Marks and written feedback are hidden until the HoD releases them. After release, view your result in the assignment details.
7. Open **Notifications** to see released-mark notices; choose a notice and click **Mark as read** when appropriate.
8. Click **Log out** when finished.

Keep submitted files in their original location because this version records file paths rather than copying files to an internal storage folder.
