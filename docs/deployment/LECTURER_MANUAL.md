# Lecturer User Manual — v1.0

1. Sign in using a Lecturer account. The window lists courses assigned to you; choose a course and assignment.
2. Click **New assignment...**, enter the requested title/deadline/maximum mark, and confirm. Check the new assignment appears in your course.
3. For an individual student's justified extension, use **Grant extension...**, select the student and revised deadline, and supply the requested reason. The late rule still applies relative to the student's updated deadline.
4. Select a submitted piece of work and use **Mark or re-mark...** to enter a valid raw mark and written feedback. For a re-mark, give a reason; changes are audited.
5. The system applies the fixed late penalty. It uses 10% of the maximum mark for each started 24-hour period after the student's deadline for up to 3 days; submissions later than 72 hours still receive feedback but have a final mark of zero.
6. Marks are **not** visible to students until the HoD approves and releases them.
7. Use **Refresh** to update the list, and **Log out** when finished.

**Version note:** Whole-class extensions are not part of this v1.0 manual. They are the requested maintenance change for v1.1.
